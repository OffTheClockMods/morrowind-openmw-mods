Infinite Arrows and Bolts (OpenMW) 1.0.0 - by Off the Clock
===========================================================
Fired arrows, bolts and thrown weapons come straight back. For OpenMW 0.50 or newer (not MWSE / Morrowind.exe).

Install: add this folder in the OpenMW Launcher (Data Files > Data Directories), then tick
OtcInfiniteArrowsBolts.omwscripts in the content list.
Settings: in the game, Options > Scripts > Infinite Arrows and Bolts (mod on, arrows and bolts, thrown weapons).
Ammo only comes back while your weapon is drawn and no menu is open, so selling or dropping ammo works as normal.
OpenMW never adds items onto the stack you have equipped, so returned arrows collect in a second stack of the same
arrows; when the equipped stack runs out, the mod equips the other one.
Nothing is written to your saves; safe to add or remove any time. Uninstall: untick the .omwscripts file.
