No Weapon and Armor Wear (OpenMW) 1.0.0 - by Off the Clock
==========================================================
Weapons, armor and shields keep their condition. For OpenMW 0.50 or newer (not MWSE / Morrowind.exe).

Install: add this folder in the OpenMW Launcher (Data Files > Data Directories), then tick
OtcNoWeaponArmorWear.omwscripts in the content list.
Settings: in the game, Options > Scripts > No Weapon and Armor Wear. Percent of normal wear per kind:
weapons 0 (default), armor and shields 0 (default), lockpicks and probes 100 (default = normal; 0 = never run out),
"Same armor wear for NPCs and creatures" off (default). 100 = the normal game, up to 1000 = ten times faster.
Faster wear stops at 1 point; the game's own wear can then break the item as usual.
Nothing is written to your saves; safe to add or remove any time.
Uninstall: untick the .omwscripts file. Gear keeps its current condition and wears normally from then on.
